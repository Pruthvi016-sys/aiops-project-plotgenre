      Tools

        How to use PlotGenre

        1. Open “Classify a plot”. Paste a plot summary of a film into the big text box, or click one of the examples.
        2. Click “Find genres”. After a moment you'll see the suggested genres.
        3. Read the result. The bars show how sure the model is for every genre. Genres with a tick are the ones it suggests.
        4. See why. Your text is shown again with the most influential words highlighted in the colour of the genre they point to.

        Tips for better results

        • Write in English and describe what happens in the story: characters, places, conflicts.
        • Longer summaries (a paragraph or more) give more reliable results than one sentence.
        • A film can belong to several genres; that's expected.

        If something goes wrong

        The button is greyed out
        Type at least 5 words.
        “The prediction service is starting…”
        The model is being loaded or updated. Wait a few seconds and click “Find genres” again. The dot at the top right turns green when it is ready.
        “The gateway service is not reachable”
        The backend is stopped. Ask the administrator to run docker compose up -d.

        The pipeline page

        This page is for the people who maintain the system. It shows each step that prepares data and trains the model, a history of runs with their result, the latest quality reports and live monitoring charts. “Run pipeline” re-runs only the steps whose inputs changed. “Retrain with newer films” adds films released after 2000 to the training data.
